The campaign

How it started

Jonathan Wang, a Kevin Love Fund Youth Council member, helped create What You Can't See. Students share photos, artwork, and personal reflections about experiences others may miss. Their stories can help someone feel less alone.

The fund

About Kevin Love Fund

Founded by NBA player Kevin Love, the fund promotes equity between mental and physical health through education, storytelling, advocacy, and resources.

Visit Kevin Love Fund Explore Education

Education, stories, support

How the fund helps

Mental health education

Curriculum helps students build emotional awareness, resilience, empathy, and self-compassion.

Stories and advocacy

Personal stories challenge stigma and make it easier to speak openly.

Community resources

Practical tools support students, educators, athletes, and families.

Next steps

Looking ahead

BSI hopes to explore bringing Kevin Love Fund educational curriculum to local elementary and middle schools in Poway Unified School District.

This is a future goal, not an existing school partnership.
